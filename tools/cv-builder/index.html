CV Builder

Tell the full story of each role, in as much detail as you like. Nothing is too small, and most of it will not end up on the page. The prompt you get at the end asks an AI assistant to pick your strongest honest points and format them as an English CV, a Japanese 職務経歴書, a 履歴書, or all three. It also builds an interview prep sheet from the same material.

Example answers are filled in so you can see how it works.
1

About youContact details and a one-line headline

▸

How you would describe yourself in one line. The AI may refine it.

Privacy: do not enter ID numbers, My Number, passport details or bank details. Date of birth and address are only needed if you want a 履歴書.
2

Work experienceOne card per job, newest first. Add as much detail as you can.

▸
How to use this section: think of a friend asking "what did you actually do there?" Write the answer. Numbers matter most: team size, users, money, time saved, volume, growth, rankings. Approximate is fine, and the AI is told never to invent figures.
3

Education and certificationsSchools, degrees, licenses, test scores

▸

Include high school only if you want it on a 履歴書. Add thesis topics, exchange programs or honours if relevant.

Include the month and year obtained. For 履歴書, 免許・資格 are listed with dates.

4

Skills and languagesEverything you can do, with honest levels

▸
Say what you did with each skill, not just its name. For example: "Python: spent 6 months building a data visualization app. I used Pandas to build dataframes and Plotly Dash to pull that data into plots, and converted them to HTML and CSS for the frontend UI." The AI uses this to judge your real level and place the skill honestly. Include how long, how deep, and how recently. A short line is fine for skills you only know a little.

Splitting by level keeps you safe in interviews, because you will be asked about anything listed.

5

Beyond your jobsProjects, awards, writing, volunteering

▸

Especially useful if you are early in your career, changing field, or have gaps to fill.

6

What you want nextYour direction and what sets you apart

▸

If your jobs went in different directions, say which story the CV should tell. If you leave this empty, the AI will choose one from your information and tell you what it chose.
